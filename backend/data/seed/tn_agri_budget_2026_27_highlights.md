# Tamil Nadu Agriculture Budget 2026-27 — highlights (compiled summary)

> Compiled in November 2026 from news reports and official documents listed at the end. This is a
> summary for quick retrieval — always verify amounts with the official budget speech / G.O.

## Two budgets in 2026-27

- **Interim Agriculture Budget 2026-27** — presented on 17 February 2026 by the then Minister for
  Agriculture–Farmers Welfare, Thiru M.R.K. Panneerselvam, with a total outlay of **Rs 47,248.24 crore**
  for agriculture and allied departments (up from Rs 45,661 crore in 2025-26 and Rs 34,220 crore in
  2021-22). He said all existing schemes would continue and that a Tamil Nadu Agroforestry Policy would be
  introduced.
- **Agriculture Budget 2026-27 (revised)** — after the 2026 Assembly election, the new Tamilaga Vettri
  Kazhagam (TVK) government headed by Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay presented its first Agriculture Budget on
  **6 August 2026**. It was presented by Agriculture–Farmers Welfare Minister **R. Vinoth**, a day after
  Finance Minister N. Marie Wilson presented the Revised Budget Estimates 2026-27. Existing agricultural
  schemes continue and new programmes were added.

## Key announcements (6 August 2026)

| Scheme / item | 2026-27 allocation / target |
|---|---|
| Tamil Nadu Soil Conservation Mission (protect soil fertility) | Rs 122.51 crore |
| Uzhavar AI scheme — AI-powered farmer app for real-time advisories | Rs 2 crore (State funds) |
| Crop insurance (premium support) | Rs 648.55 crore — 36 lakh acres, ~15 lakh farmers |
| Free three-phase electricity for agriculture | Rs 7,432.58 crore |
| Agricultural Mechanisation Scheme | Rs 227.20 crore — 24,000 farmers |
| Post-harvest assistance | 43,500 farmers |
| Millet Mission (Union + State) | Rs 63.81 crore |
| Paddy seed production subsidy (State funds, after the Centre discontinued support) | Rs 34.72 crore — 7,730 MT certified paddy seed |
| Short-term crop loans | Rs 17,000 crore |
| Special scheme for SC & ST cultivators (additional 20% subsidy) | Rs 56.72 crore |
| Pulses mission | Rs 203.64 crore (as reported in budget highlights) |
| Kuruvai Package — extended to non-delta paddy farmers (Revised Budget) | Rs 134 crore |

Other announcements reported: "Vetri Vaanmagal" scheme to train women farmers in drone technology,
"Vetri Vivasayi" awards for outstanding farmers, "Vetri Illatharasi" home gardens, introduction of
quinoa and buckwheat, and a protection package for farmlands. The Agriculture–Farmers Welfare Department
was allotted Rs 14,984 crore in the Revised Budget Estimates 2026-27.

## Related 2026 decisions

- **Crop loan waiver:** up to Rs 75,000 for every eligible farmer who borrowed from cooperative
  societies between 1 May 2025 and 31 March 2026; the scheme was later expanded with an additional
  Rs 2,842.77 crore.
- **PMFBY:** Kharif 2026-27 crop insurance notified in Tamil Nadu; crop losses due to wild-animal attacks
  are covered under PMFBY from the Kharif 2026 season (Union decision of November 2025).
