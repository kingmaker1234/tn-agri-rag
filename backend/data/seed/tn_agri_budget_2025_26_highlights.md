# Tamil Nadu Agriculture Budget 2025-26 — highlights (compiled summary)

> Compiled from the official budget speech and news coverage (sources below). Verify with the
> official speech before acting on any figure.

- Presented on **15 March 2025** by Minister for Agriculture–Farmers Welfare **M.R.K. Panneerselvam** —
  the fifth exclusive Agriculture Budget since the separate agriculture budget began in 2021-22.
- Total outlay **Rs 45,661 crore** for agriculture and allied sectors (Rs 34,221 crore in 2021-22).
- **Micro irrigation:** Rs 1,168 crore to cover 3 lakh acres with drip/sprinkler systems.
- **Kalaignarin All Village Integrated Agriculture Development Programme (KAVIADP):** continued with
  Rs 269.50 crore in 2,338 village panchayats (final phase).
- **Chief Minister's Farmers' Welfare Service Centres:** 1,000 centres equipped with modern technology to
  advise farmers; agriculture graduates receive a 30% subsidy to set them up.
- **Hill farmers development scheme:** minor millets, vegetables, inputs, farm mechanisation and value
  addition for hill-area farmers, with Kisan Credit Cards for beneficiaries.
- **International exposure visit:** pioneer farmers to be sent to countries such as Israel, the Netherlands
  and Thailand (Rs 3 crore for 150 farmers, as reported).
- **Agroforestry:** a Tamil Nadu Agroforestry Policy to promote commercially valuable trees such as
  sandalwood, red sanders, mahogany and rosewood.
- **Value addition:** a scheme to support 100 agricultural value-addition and processing centres (notified
  later in 2025).
- In the previous financial year, 1.81 lakh pump sets and 1.86 lakh agricultural power connections were
  provided to farmers.
- **Chief Minister's Mannuyir Kaathu Mannuyir Kaappom scheme** (introduced 2024-25 with Rs 178 crore for
  about 21.35 lakh farmers) continued in 2025-26.
